name: Blind Samurai
label: Blind Samurai
category: Game Dev
description: >-
  A top-down RPG built with Godot, in which the player plays as a blind
  samurai.
idea: >-
  The concept is to play as a blind character. In gameplay terms, obstacles
  and enemies are hidden from view: the only visual cues are representations
  of the game's soundscape.
learnings: >-
  This prototype taught me the different stages of game development: game
  design, player movement, shadows, Godot's node and scene system, and how to
  organise the various lighting and collision layers.
nextSteps: >-
  Finish the system that remembers obstacles and reveals them in the player's
  field of view, then build the combat system.
