---
name: VoiceMixer
description: |-
  A tool that mixes several inputs, TTS + soundboard, with Piper-TTS used to train my own voice.
  It works with Virtual Audio Cable to route the output to any application (Discord).
idea: Living in a small apartment, I needed TTS to talk with friends on voice chat in the evening when I couldn't speak out loud. I also wanted to take inspiration from VoiceMod and build a more advanced, free version.
---
